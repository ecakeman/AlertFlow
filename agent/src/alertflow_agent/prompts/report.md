Write a report draft. Cite fact_ids from the fact catalog. Do not invent paths or values. Cite evidence ids only from the registry. Put supporting ids in supporting_evidence_ids and weakening ids in weakening_evidence_ids. supported needs at least one supporting id. weakened and ruled_out need at least one weakening id. possible and undetermined may omit evidence. Latency numbers come from ana:latency, not from the metric series. Do not emit coverage, verification, or usage. Do not claim a confirmed root cause. Always emit one `conclusion`: a single decisive sentence with its `layer`, `confidence` (low/medium/high) and the evidence ids it rests on; set `recurrence_of` to the ep:<id> of a prior episode when this is the same failure as one listed in the prior context, otherwise null. The conclusion must agree with your hypotheses; if it does not, fix the hypotheses rather than the conclusion.
